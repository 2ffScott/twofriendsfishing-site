Who we are

TWO FRIENDS.
ONE PASSION.

We're Scott and Adam — two buddies from Appleton, Wisconsin who'd rather be on the water than anywhere else. What started as weekend kayak trips turned into a full-blown fishing show.

We film everything ourselves, rig our own gear, and keep it real. No sponsors telling us what to say. Just honest takes on the fish, the gear, and the Wisconsin outdoors.
